        Controls & conventions

        • Drag — rotate camera
        • Scroll — zoom
        • Right-drag — pan
        • Altitude is in Moon radii (RM = 1 unit ≈ 1737 km). 0.03 ≈ a 50 km mapping orbit.
        • Inclination = orbital plane tilt from equator. 0° = equatorial, 90° = polar.
        • RAAN = where the orbit crosses the equator going north.
        • ω is angular speed along the orbit; α applies a constant change to ω.
