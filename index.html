Ground stations & satellites

Drop ground points (lat/lon + field-of-view half-angle) to project view cones into space. Add satellites with configurable orbit, velocity, and acceleration — satellites brighten while inside any cone, and the cone brightens with them.

      Controls & conventions
      • Drag — rotate camera
      • Scroll — zoom
      • Right-drag — pan
      • Altitude is in Earth radii (RE = 1 unit). 0.08 ≈ LEO.
      • Inclination = orbital plane tilt from equator. 0° = equatorial, 90° = polar.
      • RAAN = where the orbit crosses the equator going north.
      • ω is angular speed along the orbit; α applies a constant change to ω.
